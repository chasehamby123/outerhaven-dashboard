Industry coverage

Sector context shapes strategy, structure and capital.

We focus on sectors where the nature of the asset, business model and capital base materially affect how a transaction should be positioned and which counterparties are relevant.

Coverage areas

Focused where our relationships and transaction context are most useful.

These are coverage areas, not claims of universal sector leadership. We engage selectively where we can combine strategic understanding with a credible capital or counterparty universe.

01

Real Estate, Lodging & Leisure

Development, hospitality, operating real estate and asset-backed situations requiring equity, debt, structured capital or strategic partnership.

Typical situations
  • Hospitality and resort development
  • Mixed-use and commercial projects
  • Asset recapitalizations
  • Strategic investment or ownership change
02

Business & Professional Services

Recurring-revenue, outsourced-services and professional-services businesses where scale, consolidation or ownership transition creates transaction opportunities.

Typical situations
  • Growth and acquisition capital
  • Buy-and-build strategies
  • Founder liquidity
  • Strategic sale or recapitalization
03

Consumer & Multi-Site

Franchise, hospitality, branded consumer and location-based businesses where unit economics, expansion and ownership structure shape the capital need.

Typical situations
  • Multi-location expansion
  • Franchise and platform acquisitions
  • Minority growth capital
  • Ownership transition
04

Infrastructure & Energy

Physical and digital infrastructure, project finance and capital-intensive opportunities where long-duration economics and financing structure are central to the transaction.

Typical situations
  • Project and development financing
  • Infrastructure equity
  • Private credit
  • Strategic joint ventures
05

Technology & Digital

Software, digital platforms and technology-enabled services where growth profile, recurring revenue and investor fit shape the capital strategy.

Typical situations
  • Growth equity
  • Strategic investment
  • Acquisition capital
  • Shareholder liquidity
06

Financial & Specialty Services

Financial platforms, specialty finance and capital-intensive service businesses where balance-sheet structure and institutional capital relationships matter.

Typical situations
  • Growth and regulatory capital
  • Structured financing
  • Strategic partnerships
  • Recapitalization
