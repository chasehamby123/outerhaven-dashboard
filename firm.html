The firm

Independent by design. Relationship-led in practice.

Outerhaven is an independent advisory firm built around partner accountability, disciplined transaction review and selective use of capital relationships.

Our approach

The relationship is part of the transaction.

Capital access only creates value when the opportunity is prepared and the counterparty has a credible reason to engage.

Outerhaven keeps transaction review, relationship strategy and execution close to the partners responsible for the work. We also recognize the role of the originator, sponsor or advisor who brought the opportunity into the process.

Principles

How we work.

Our operating model is designed to protect the quality of the transaction and the relationships around it.

Selective engagement

Not every opportunity should become a capital process. We engage where the transaction is ready for a serious conversation or can be made ready.

Partner accountability

Origination, diligence and counterparty strategy remain close to the partners responsible for the relationship.

Relationship protection

Originating relationships and existing advisory roles remain recognized throughout the process.

Clear information

We distinguish what is known, what is represented and what still requires diligence before it reaches a counterparty.

Focused outreach

We prefer a smaller number of relevant conversations over broad distribution without a clear investment case.

Independence

Advice first. Access second.

Our value is not simply knowing capital. It is understanding when a relationship should be used, why the opportunity belongs in front of it and how to keep the process credible.
