The firm

Strategic Advisory & Capital Formation Partners.

Outerhaven is a partner-led advisory firm working with private companies, sponsors, asset owners and advisors on strategic transactions, capital structure and private-market financing.

Our purpose

Independent advice around the decisions that shape a transaction.

Important transactions rarely begin with a single financing product. They begin with a strategic question: whether to raise capital, pursue an acquisition, change ownership, refinance, bring in a partner or restructure the capital base.

Outerhaven is built to work across that decision. We help frame the alternatives, prepare the situation and determine which capital or strategic counterparties have a credible reason to engage.

What makes us different

A focused advisory model built around judgment and relationships.

Our model is designed for situations where senior attention, discretion and the quality of the counterparty matter more than broad distribution.

Partner-led execution

Strategic review, capital strategy and relationship deployment remain close to the partners accountable for the mandate.

Independent perspective

We start with the client’s objective and evaluate multiple paths rather than forcing every situation into a predefined capital product.

Private-market connectivity

Our relationship coverage spans family offices, institutional investors, sponsors, private credit, strategic investors and specialist capital providers.

Selective engagement

We prefer a focused set of counterparties with a credible reason to engage over broad, indiscriminate distribution.

Originator alignment

Where a transaction comes through an advisor or originator, that relationship remains recognized throughout the process.

Our model

Advice, capital and execution in one connected process.

The strategic decision informs the financing. The financing structure informs the investor universe. The investor universe informs execution. We keep those decisions connected.

Prepared before distributed.
