The firm

Strategic Advisory & Capital Formation Partners.

Outerhaven is a partner-led advisory firm working across private-market transactions, strategic alternatives and capital formation. We combine transaction judgment with selective access to family offices, institutions, strategic investors and specialist capital providers.

Our model

Advice first. Capital second. Execution throughout.

A capital relationship is most valuable when the strategic objective, structure and investment case are already clear. Outerhaven is built around that sequence.

We work with owners, sponsors and advisors to define the situation, prepare the transaction and determine which counterparties have a credible reason to engage. Senior involvement remains close to the work from initial review through active execution.

What defines us

Independent judgment. Selective execution.

Our operating model is designed around a smaller number of relevant conversations, stronger preparation and clear accountability for the relationships used in the process.

Strategic context first

We begin with the decision the client is trying to make, not with a predefined financing product or distribution list.

Partner accountability

Transaction review, capital strategy and counterparty judgment remain close to the partners responsible for the mandate.

Situation-specific capital

The capital universe is selected around the structure, sector, geography, size and strategic context of the opportunity.

Relationship discipline

We prefer focused engagement with counterparties who have a credible reason to participate over broad undifferentiated outreach.

Originator alignment

Where an opportunity comes through an advisor or originator, that relationship remains recognized and visible throughout the process.

Operating platform

One firm. Three connected layers.

Strategic advice defines the decision. Capital formation defines the financing path. The Partner Platform gives qualified originators and advisors a structured route into that process.
