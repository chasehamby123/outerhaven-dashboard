Stage 3 · Investor preparation

Build the package investors actually receive.

Once the readiness work is complete, the opportunity moves into a focused preparation stage. The goal is a clean, consistent package that can be reviewed without forcing an investor to reconstruct the story.

Opportunity overviewConcise transaction summary, company context and capital requirement.
Investment caseWhy the opportunity exists, what the capital enables and the core value drivers.
Financial profileRelevant historical and projected financial information presented consistently.
Transaction structureCapital type, proposed structure, use of funds and key transaction considerations.
Supporting diligenceOrganized source documents and a clear list of remaining diligence items.
Investor profileCapital requirements and characteristics used to route the opportunity appropriately.
