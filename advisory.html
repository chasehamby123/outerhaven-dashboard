Execution model

One process from assessment to engagement.

The exact scope depends on the mandate, but the sequence remains disciplined: understand the objective, structure the situation, prepare the information and only then use capital relationships.

Assess

Clarify the strategic objective, transaction economics, stakeholders, constraints and viable paths.

Structure

Determine the transaction or financing structure most appropriate for the situation and the desired outcome.

Position

Organize the materials, diligence and investment case so relevant counterparties can make an informed first decision.

Execute

Coordinate outreach, introductions, information requests and next steps through the active process.

Working alongside advisors

Incremental coverage without disrupting the existing relationship.

Outerhaven can work alongside investment bankers, advisors and originators where the client process is already controlled but additional capital or buyer coverage would improve the outcome.

The originating relationship remains visible. Our role is defined up front around the specific counterparties, capital need or execution gap we are being asked to address.
