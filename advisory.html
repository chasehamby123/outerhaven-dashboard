Advisory

Preparation, counterparty judgment and access in the same process.

Outerhaven supports transactions where the quality of the counterparties and the preparation behind the outreach both matter. Our work spans capital raising, strategic transactions, structured finance, project and development capital, incremental buyer coverage and transaction readiness.

Service lines

Six areas of work. A consistent standard underneath them.

The exact scope depends on the engagement. In some situations Outerhaven is involved early in preparation and diligence. In others, an existing advisor already runs the process and our role is to add relevant counterparty coverage. We define that role before using the relationship.

01

Capital Raising

Private equity and growth-capital situations where a sponsor or company needs focused connectivity to family offices, institutions and strategic capital. The work begins with understanding the raise, not announcing it to a database.

•
02

M&A & Strategic Transactions

Buyer coverage and strategic introductions across acquisitions, ownership transitions, recapitalizations and full or partial sales. Counterparty selection is shaped by transaction logic, not just buyer size.

•
03

Debt & Structured Capital

Situations where a conventional equity process is not the only answer. We help identify relevant lenders, structured-capital providers and flexible counterparties where the transaction supports that approach.

•
04

Project & Development Capital

Real-asset, infrastructure, hospitality and development opportunities where the sponsor, asset, capital stack and execution plan all need to be understood before the capital conversation is credible.

•
05

Incremental Buyer Coverage

Additional relationship reach for investment bankers, advisors and originators who already control the client process but want another path into relevant institutions, family offices or strategic buyers.

•
06

Transaction Readiness

Review of materials, financial context, diligence gaps, decision makers and the questions a serious counterparty is likely to ask. The goal is to prevent avoidable friction before it reaches the market.

Counterparty strategy

The first name approached can matter as much as the fiftieth name available.

We think about the order of outreach, the reason each relationship belongs in the process and what information that counterparty needs to make a useful first decision. That is different from simply maximizing distribution.

RelevanceA specific reason for the conversation.
SequenceNot every buyer should hear the same thing at the same time.
OwnershipThe originating relationship stays visible.
Working method

A transaction should become clearer as more people touch it.

Outerhaven's internal process is designed to reduce ambiguity as a transaction progresses. Opportunity details, materials, diligence, counterparty interest and next steps are organized in one controlled record so the relationship does not depend on memory or scattered messages.

Scope first

Define what Outerhaven is responsible for, what the existing advisor or originator owns, and what a successful next step actually looks like.

Diligence before outreach

Understand the transaction well enough to know which questions remain open and which statements can be supported.

Counterparty selection

Use relationship knowledge to narrow the field instead of treating access as a reason to contact everyone.

Controlled coordination

Track who has seen what, where interest exists, what information is outstanding and who owns the next action.

Private credentials

Transaction-specific examples and current activity can be discussed in direct conversations where confidentiality permits.

If the transaction is important enough to reach institutional capital, it is important enough to prepare properly.
