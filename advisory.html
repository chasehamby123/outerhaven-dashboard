Capabilities

Strategic advice and capital solutions for private-market transactions.

We help clients evaluate strategic alternatives, shape capital structure, prepare transactions and engage relevant capital and strategic counterparties. Our practices are integrated so the advice, financing strategy and execution remain connected.

01 · Strategic & M&A Advisory

Advice around consequential strategic decisions.

We support private companies, sponsors, asset owners and advisors as they evaluate and execute transactions where structure, timing and counterparty selection can materially influence the result.

Strategic Alternatives

Assessment of available paths before a transaction process begins, including ownership change, partnership, recapitalization, acquisition or continued independent growth.

Buy-Side & Acquisition Advisory

Opportunity assessment, counterparty approach, transaction framing and coordination around acquisitions and strategic investments.

Sell-Side & Ownership Transition

Preparation and selective buyer coverage around founder liquidity, succession, divestiture and ownership-change situations.

Joint Ventures & Strategic Partnerships

Partner identification and transaction support where a strategic relationship may be more appropriate than a conventional financing or outright sale.

02 · Capital Formation

Financing strategies tailored to the business and the objective.

We help define the financing need, evaluate alternatives, position the opportunity and coordinate a focused private-capital process across relevant debt, equity and structured-capital providers.

Equity

Growth & Minority Capital

Capital for expansion, acquisitions, shareholder liquidity and long-term growth where founders or sponsors want to preserve strategic flexibility.

Credit

Private Credit & Debt

Financing alternatives around acquisitions, refinancing, growth, liquidity and other situations requiring private lenders or specialist credit capital.

Structured

Structured & Hybrid Capital

Preferred equity, hybrid structures and bespoke financing where the right answer sits between conventional senior debt and common equity.

Projects

Project & Development Capital

Equity, debt and structured capital around hospitality, real estate, infrastructure and development-led opportunities.

03 · Capital Structure Advisory

Evaluate financing options before the market decides for you.

Capital structure decisions influence strategic flexibility, ownership, cost of capital and execution risk. We help frame the available alternatives and the tradeoffs around each path.

Capital Structure Assessment

Review of the existing debt and equity mix, near-term requirements and strategic constraints around the capital base.

Refinancing & Liquidity

Assessment of refinancing, private-credit and liquidity alternatives where timing, maturity or growth needs require new capital.

Recapitalization

Evaluation of new debt, preferred capital, minority equity or ownership changes designed to reset the balance sheet or create liquidity.

Acquisition Financing

Capital strategy around acquisitions, sponsor-led transactions and strategic combinations requiring coordinated financing.

04 · Private Capital Solutions

Focused access to the private-capital universe.

Our relationship coverage is used selectively. We identify the investors, lenders, sponsors and strategic counterparties whose mandate, structure and decision process fit the opportunity.

Family Office & Institutional Coverage

Targeted engagement with private and institutional capital providers based on mandate fit, geography, sector, structure and check size.

Direct & Co-Investment Capital

Capital for single-asset and direct investment opportunities where investors are evaluating a specific company, acquisition or project.

Sponsor Solutions

Capital coverage for funded and independent sponsors around acquisitions, co-investment, portfolio-company financing and strategic opportunities.

Strategic Investor Engagement

Identification and approach of corporate or strategic capital where commercial alignment matters alongside financial return.

Execution principles

Independent judgment. Focused execution.

The strongest process is not necessarily the broadest. We prefer a clearly defined objective, a prepared transaction and a smaller number of counterparties with a real reason to engage.
