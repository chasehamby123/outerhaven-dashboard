Operating Standards

Competence is visible in how a transaction is handled.

Our operating model is built to preserve the quality of both sides of the relationship. That means controlling information, setting clear next steps and avoiding outreach that damages buyer trust or the originating relationship.

01

Relationship Protection

Originating relationships remain identifiable and protected through the process. We do not position Outerhaven to bypass the source of the opportunity.

02

Confidentiality

Opportunity details, buyer identities, thesis information and transaction materials are handled inside controlled workflows rather than public submission pages.

03

Diligence Before Distribution

We seek enough context to understand the transaction before using capital relationships or coordinating an introduction.

04

Focused Outreach

Buyer fit is based on current mandate information and transaction relevance, not the size of a generic contact list.
