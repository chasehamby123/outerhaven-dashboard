Method

The process is deliberate before it becomes visible.

Outerhaven uses a consistent operating sequence so an opportunity is not simply forwarded from one relationship to the next. The transaction is understood first, diligence gaps are identified, and counterparty strategy follows from the facts of the situation.

01 · Define

Understand the transaction

Clarify the objective, counterparties, capital need, structure, timeline and decision makers.

02 · Review

Diligence the opportunity

Review materials, financial context, sponsorship and the issues a serious counterparty is likely to raise.

03 · Position

Prepare the case

Organize the information so the transaction can be understood quickly without overselling what has not been proven.

04 · Select

Choose the relationship path

Determine which counterparties are worth approaching, in what order and for what specific reason.

05 · Coordinate

Run the conversation

Track introductions, questions, materials, next steps and relationship ownership through the active process.
