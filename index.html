Method

The process is deliberate before it becomes visible.

A transaction should become clearer as more people touch it. Outerhaven uses a consistent operating sequence so outreach follows from the facts of the situation rather than from the size of a contact file.

01Define

Clarify the objective, capital need, structure, timeline, counterparties and decision makers.

02Review

Understand materials, financial context, sponsorship and the issues a serious counterparty will raise.

03Position

Organize the information so the transaction can be understood quickly without overselling what has not been proven.

04Select

Choose the counterparties worth approaching, in what order and for what specific reason.

05Coordinate

Manage introductions, questions, materials, next steps and relationship ownership through the active process.
