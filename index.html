Operating Principles

Built around fit, discretion, and relationship protection.

Outerhaven is designed to make focused introductions without turning opportunity distribution into a public marketplace.

Can I submit a deal from the public website?

No. Create an account or sign in first, then submit the opportunity inside the partner dashboard.

Does signing up give me internal Outerhaven access?

No. Public registration creates a partner account only. Internal team access remains separate.

Are opportunities distributed broadly?

No. Opportunities are reviewed and matched to relevant buyer mandates before outreach or introduction.

Can I track what happens after submission?

Yes. The partner dashboard tracks review, matching, buyer interest, engagement, and the next steps around your submission.

Have an opportunity worth putting in front of qualified capital?

Create your partner account, submit it privately, and let Outerhaven determine where the strongest mandate fit exists.
