Operating Principles

Built around fit, discretion, and relationship protection.

Outerhaven is designed to make the right introductions, not the most introductions. That means understanding where an opportunity came from, who controls the relationship, and whether the buyer mandate is genuinely aligned.

Do you distribute every opportunity broadly?

No. Opportunities are reviewed and matched to relevant mandates before outreach.

Can originators work through the platform?

Approved partners can use the private originator portal to submit opportunities, complete diligence, and track matching progress.

Can capital partners share specific criteria?

Yes. Sector, geography, check size, and preferred structures can be captured as a live mandate for future matching.

Are introductions automatic?

No. A match score helps prioritize fit, but Outerhaven reviews the opportunity before a relationship is introduced.
